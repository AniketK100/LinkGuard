Frequently Asked Questions

How does LinkGuard ensure sub-100ms redirection speed?

LinkGuard uses a Redis cache-aside architecture. Short link lookups resolve from memory in under 10ms with database fallback.

Is visitor IP data kept private and GDPR compliant?

Yes. IPs are anonymized via SHA-256 hashing with a daily rotating salt before analytics logging. No raw IPs are stored.

Can I customize QR code colors?

Yes. Our QR Studio lets you customize foreground/background colors and export in PNG or SVG formats.
